# BrainDump

A cozy little website for getting tasks out of your head and making time to focus.
(https://braindump00.netlify.app/)

## About

BrainDump brings a task list, weekly planner, and focus timer into one simple study space. It’s made for those days when you have a lot to do and need somewhere to start.

I went with a soft cream theme and an arched focus room to give it a calm, comfortable feel.

## Features

- Add, edit, complete, and delete tasks.
- Organise your week with the planner.
- Start a focus timer and take breaks between sessions.
- Keep your tasks and preferences saved in your browser.

## Using it

Visit **https://braindump00.netlify.app/** and start adding your tasks. There’s nothing to install and no account needed.

Your data stays in the browser you use. It won’t sync across devices, and clearing the site’s browser data will remove your saved tasks.

## Built with

- HTML
- CSS
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Hosted on Netlify

No frameworks or backend—just three main files: `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js`.
